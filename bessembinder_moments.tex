\noindent\footnotesize
\textbf{Sample.} Daily Compustat returns, US common stock, trfd-recovered
(never-dividend securities get $\mathrm{trfd}=1$). Filters:
$\mathrm{tpci}='0'$ (common), $\mathrm{prcstd}\in\{3,4,10\}$, valid price/currency,
$\mathrm{excntry}='\mathrm{USA}'$, $\mathrm{primary\_sec}=1$. No exchange screen
($\mathrm{exch\_main}$ \emph{not} applied), so OTC-coded rows remain.

\smallskip
\noindent
\textbf{Columns.}
\emph{Raw} --- uncorrected.
\emph{ret\,$<$\,10} --- Raw, dropping $\mathrm{ret}>10$.
\emph{Bessembinder} --- Section~6 decimal corrections $+$ Section~8 deletions
($N$ falls to 70.1M).
\emph{Bess.\ \& ret\,$<$\,10} --- Bessembinder, dropping $\mathrm{ret}>10$.

\smallskip
\noindent
\textbf{Notes.} The clip removes only the extreme tail ($p1$--$p99$ unchanged
within each correction regime) but collapses the variance-driven moments.
Bessembinder caps Max at 1{,}559 vs.\ 11.1M raw (Section~8 kills the giant
penny$\to$trade spikes); 596 residual $\mathrm{ret}>10$ obs still dominate Std,
Skewness, and Excess Kurtosis until clipped.
